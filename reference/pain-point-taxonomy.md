# Pain Point Taxonomy — Independent Venue Operators

A structured map of where independent venues feel pain, organised by department. Each entry names the pain, the typical workaround, and the failure mode of that workaround.

---

## Finance & COGS

| Pain | Workaround | Workaround failure |
|---|---|---|
| Don't know actual food cost until end of month stocktake | Manual stocktake + spreadsheet | Data is always stale; decisions get made on last month's numbers |
| Supplier prices change without notice | Re-check invoices occasionally | Price drift goes unnoticed for months; margin erodes invisibly |
| Recipe costs never updated after initial setup | Static spreadsheet | Costing decisions made on outdated ingredient prices |
| Can't tell which menu items are profitable | Gut feel / rough calculation | High-volume items may be sold at a loss without knowing |
| Cash flow unpredictable week to week | Personal bank account buffers | Owner personally absorbs shortfalls; no visibility into pattern |
| Accountant sees data 30–60 days after the fact | Quarterly catch-up meetings | No real-time decision support |

## Labour & Rostering

| Pain | Workaround | Workaround failure |
|---|---|---|
| Last-minute staff callouts | WhatsApp group scramble | Owner ends up covering the shift; no audit trail |
| No real-time view of labour cost vs revenue | Mental estimate | Overstaffing on slow days is only visible after payroll |
| Award rate complexity (AU/UK) | Payroll software (Xero, MYOB) that flags errors late | Underpayments accumulate; compliance risk |
| Roster built from memory of staff availability | Owner's mental model | Scheduling conflicts, favouritism perception, staff dissatisfaction |
| Casual staff availability changes weekly | Group chat updates | Roster distributed, then renegotiated ad hoc |
| High staff turnover means constant retraining | Senior staff informally train new hires | Inconsistent training; service quality varies |

---

## Suppliers & Purchasing

| Pain | Workaround | Workaround failure |
|---|---|---|
| No centralised view of what was ordered vs delivered | Paper delivery dockets filed (or not filed) | Discrepancies between order and delivery go unbilled |
| Reordering based on gut feel, not par levels | Walk the cool room and write a list | Over-ordering leads to waste; under-ordering leads to 86'd items |
| Invoices arrive in multiple formats (email, paper, WhatsApp photo) | Manually enter into accounting software | Time-consuming; transcription errors; owner often does this themselves at night |
| No supplier price history tracked | Nothing | Can't negotiate from data; can't identify price creep |
| Multiple suppliers for same category with no comparison | Loyalty / inertia | Higher prices stay in place because switching cost feels too high |

---

## Compliance & Admin

| Pain | Workaround | Workaround failure |
|---|---|---|
| Food safety logs need to be completed daily | Paper log on clipboard | Often filled retrospectively; fails during an inspection |
| Staff certifications (food handling, RSA, first aid) expire | Owner remembers or doesn't | Expired certs create liability; discovered during incident or audit |
| Insurance renewal dates missed | Email reminder from broker | Policy lapses or wrong coverage retained by default |
| Incident reporting done inconsistently | Paper form in the back office | No audit trail; liability exposure in claims |
| Regulatory requirements vary and change | Industry association newsletters | Changes missed; non-compliance goes unnoticed |

---

## Menu & Pricing

| Pain | Workaround | Workaround failure |
|---|---|---|
| Menu prices set by feel / competitor benchmarking | Walk past competitors, look at their menus | Price is not connected to cost; margin unknown |
| Specials priced on the fly | Chef decides in the moment | High food cost specials reduce weekly margin without visibility |
| Menu not reviewed after COGS changes | Annual or biannual menu reprint | Selling items at a loss for months before it's noticed |
| Seasonal ingredient cost spikes not anticipated | Adjust when it's already happening | Reactive margin loss on key menu items |

---

## Technology & Systems

| Pain | Workaround | Workaround failure |
|---|---|---|
| Multiple disconnected tools (POS, accounting, rostering, comms) | Manual data transfer or just ignoring some | No single source of truth; decisions made from partial data |
| POS data not used beyond daily takings | Check end-of-day report, file it | Trend data, item-level sales, and table turn data goes unused |
| New tools require setup time the operator doesn't have | Trial, abandon, trial again | Operators stuck with inadequate legacy tools |
| No structured onboarding when tools are adopted | Watch a YouTube video | Low adoption, features unused, cancellation after 3 months |
| Owner is the only person who knows how everything works | Nothing | Single point of failure; owner can never fully step away |
