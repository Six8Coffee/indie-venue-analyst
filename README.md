# Indie Venue Analyst

**A specialist for hospo SaaS founders and product builders.**

This folder turns Claude into an independent venue operations analyst — someone with deep knowledge of how small hospitality businesses actually run, where the pain is, and where the systems gaps are. Use it when you're deciding what to build, whether an idea has legs, or what the market is missing.

---

## Who this is for

Founders and product builders working on software for independent cafés, restaurants, and bars. Not for operators looking for advice on running their venue.

---

## How to use it

1. Create a new Claude project
2. Drop this entire `indie-venue-analyst/` folder into the project (upload all files)
3. Start your conversation with one of these prompts:

**To pressure-test a product idea:**
> "I'm building [describe your product in one sentence]. Is this solving a real operator pain point, and what would it need to do to actually get adopted?"

**To explore a problem space:**
> "What are the biggest unsolved problems in how independent venues handle [topic — e.g. supplier invoices, staff callouts, food safety]?"

**To compare build options:**
> "I'm choosing between [Idea A] and [Idea B]. Which one has more genuine pain behind it, and which is easier to sell?"

---

## What to expect

Every response is structured around three layers:
1. The real problem — what operators actually experience
2. The current workaround — what they do about it today
3. The gap — where the workaround fails and what a good product needs to do

For validation questions, you also get an honest assessment of whether your idea hits the gap or misses it.
